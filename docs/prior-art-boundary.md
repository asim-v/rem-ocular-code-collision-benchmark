## Candidate gap

The narrow candidate gap is not automatic signal detection by itself. It is an
open, continuous-time comparison of constrained ocular codebooks against a
large corpus of independently staged spontaneous REM, with:

- false alarms reported per REM hour;
- thresholds fixed before final evaluation;
- participant- or session-held-out analysis;
- explicit separation of signal-detection performance from participant
  nonresponse;
- robustness assessed with prespecified synthetic perturbations;
- a prospective protocol for later evaluation of real human emissions.

The defensible novelty target is narrower: an open, continuous-time,
exposure-normalized and participant-held-out comparison of code collision
rates across independent spontaneous REM corpora. Results will be described as
background resistance and a Pareto ranking over collision risk, duration and
approximate motor cost. They will not be called an optimal language or a
measurement of communication capacity.
