# External replication of the high-recovery FROC crossing

## Status and rationale

This is a new study generated by the completed sleep-cassette result. It is
not a secondary continuation of the original confirmatory sequence.

The sleep-cassette primary test at 90% matched synthetic recovery failed. A
prespecified but gated 95% operating point showed a large descriptive reversal:
`sync8_c0` produced 27.155 background events per eligible REM hour and
`iso8_matched` produced 52.571. The rate ratio was 0.517. Because the primary
gate failed, that result is only a hypothesis about a crossing FROC tradeoff.

The Sleep-EDF sleep-telemetry waveforms have not been downloaded or opened.
This protocol, gate, code and source hashes are committed before that access.

## Primary question

Among the 22 prespecified placebo nights in Sleep-EDF sleep-telemetry, does
`sync8_c0` produce fewer spontaneous REM background collisions than
`iso8_matched` when the two codes are rematched to at least 95% recovery of the
same paired synthetic engineering injections?

This tests an engineering tradeoff in uninstructed EOG. It does not estimate
human code-production sensitivity, dream communication accuracy or anomalous
information transfer.

## Frozen cohort and processing

The primary external cohort is the placebo night from each of the 22
sleep-telemetry participants identified in the existing frozen manifest.
Temazepam nights remain sealed. The detector, preprocessing, REM masks,
trailing robust calibration, 1.5-MAD amplitude gate, one-second nonmaximum
suppression, paired injection generator, 20-anchor maximum and complete zero
score floor are unchanged from the amended SC benchmark.

Only combined result tables are used for inference. The inferential script
does not reopen physiological recordings.

## Primary estimand and threshold rule

The sole primary operating point is 95% synthetic recovery at amplitude 4 MAD
and 15% interval jitter. Each code receives the same anchors and perturbation
draws. Within sleep-telemetry, each code threshold is the largest observed
synthetic score recovering at least 95% of injections. Background counts do
not influence threshold selection.

The primary estimand is the synchronized minus isochronous difference in
background detections per eligible expert-scored REM hour. A 50,000-replicate
participant-clustered bootstrap resamples participants with all of their
exposure, candidates and injections, and reselects both thresholds inside each
replicate.

External replication requires both:

1. the upper limit of the two-sided participant-clustered 95% interval is
   below zero;
2. the point rate ratio is at most 0.70.

Failure of either rule is a failed replication, not evidence of equivalence.
There is one primary test and no optional stopping.

## Secondary transport description

For description only, the SC 95% thresholds are frozen at 0.5434436374 for
`sync8_c0` and 0.4520665809 for `iso8_matched`. Their recovery and background
rates in sleep-telemetry will be reported to show absolute threshold transport.
This calculation cannot replace the within-corpus matched primary test.

## Ordered external gate

CAP normal controls remain unopened. CAP may be downloaded only if both ST
replication rules pass and a separate CAP analysis gate is committed first.
If ST fails, the external sequence stops. Temazepam nights require their own
future protocol regardless of the ST result.

## Permitted conclusion

Success would support a high-recovery background-resistance tradeoff across a
second acquisition cohort. It would not rescue the failed SC 90% primary
claim, establish a generally superior code, demonstrate human usability or
provide evidence for psi.
